# ClawdIntern.sol — evm-audit-defi-staking findings (2026-08-08)

Contract: `packages/foundry/contracts/ClawdIntern.sol` (vesting/stream escrow; checklist's reward-calculation, cooldown-exploitation, accounting, and state-machine items mapped onto the term machine).

## [STAKE-1] `reassignIntern` bypasses the reappointment cooldown
**Severity**: Low
**Category**: evm-audit-defi-staking
**Location**: `reassignIntern()` (ClawdIntern.sol:240) / `openTerm()` cooldown check (ClawdIntern.sol:133-134)
**Description**: `closeTerm`/`cancelTerm` stamp `lastTermEnd[t.intern]`, and `openTerm` enforces `cooldown` against that mapping. `reassignIntern` redirects a closed term's remaining stream to `newIntern` but never touches `lastTermEnd` — neither copying the old intern's stamp to `newIntern` nor stamping `newIntern` when the stream it now owns was the one that just ended. The result: the address actually collecting the just-ended term's payout has `lastTermEnd == 0` and can be appointed to a brand-new term in the same block, while still receiving the old stream. Cooldown is the checklist's lock-time mechanism here (analog of "staking for others reduces lock time" [beirao LS-01]): its point is that the same beneficiary can't run back-to-back terms. Both `reassignIntern` and `openTerm` are `onlyOwner`, so no third party can exploit this alone — but Phase 0's stated model is that owner discretion is *publicly auditable*, and the honest way to waive cooldown is `setParams(_cooldown=0)`, which emits `ParamsUpdated` for everyone to see. The reassign path achieves the same waiver without any param-change event, so it quietly weakens the self-binding property the cooldown is documented to provide. (Cooldown is also per-address and therefore Sybil-soft in general — a fresh EOA dodges it with owner cooperation — which caps this at Low.)
**Proof of Concept**: Confirmed with a forge test (run against the repo, then deleted): open term for `intern`, warp past end, `closeTerm(markIn*10)` (full-budget stream). `openTerm(intern, …)` reverts `InternOnCooldown`. `reassignIntern(id, heir)` then `openTerm(heir, …)` succeeds immediately — `heir` holds the old term's full streaming payout and a new active term simultaneously, zero cooldown observed.
**Recommendation**: In `reassignIntern`, carry the cooldown with the stream: `if (lastTermEnd[newIntern] < lastTermEnd[t.intern]) lastTermEnd[newIntern] = lastTermEnd[t.intern];` (max-preserving so a reassign can never *shorten* an existing stamp). Alternatively, document explicitly that cooldown binds addresses, not beneficiaries, and that reassign is a cooldown escape hatch.

## [STAKE-2] Terms with `streamLen <= SLASH_DELAY` are permanently unslashable — no floor enforced on `streamLength`
**Severity**: Low
**Category**: evm-audit-defi-staking
**Location**: `slash()` (ClawdIntern.sol:218-233), `setParams()` (ClawdIntern.sol:250-256), `constructor` (ClawdIntern.sol:108-118)
**Description**: `slash` computes `returned = t.payout - _vested(t)` and reverts `NothingToSlash` when it is zero, and separately reverts `SlashTooEarly` before `closedAt + SLASH_DELAY` (2 days). If a term was opened while `streamLength <= SLASH_DELAY` (the constructor and `setParams` only require `_streamLength != 0`), the stream fully vests before the slash window ever opens: before the delay `returned` may be nonzero but `SlashTooEarly` blocks; from `elapsed >= streamLen` onward `returned == 0` blocks. Note the check order makes the pre-delay window even narrower than it looks — `NothingToSlash` is evaluated *before* `SlashTooEarly`, so once the short stream vests, every call reverts `NothingToSlash` regardless of the delay. The slash mechanism — the contract's only post-close recovery tool against a manipulated markOut or agreement breach — is silently dead for the term's whole lifetime. This only ever favors the intern (they keep 100%), and it takes an owner misconfiguration via `setParams`, hence Low rather than Medium. It is the mirror image of the documented guarantee "the intern always vests at least SLASH_DELAY/streamLen of an honest payout": at `streamLen <= SLASH_DELAY` that ratio is >= 1 and the owner's side of the mechanism vanishes with no warning.
**Proof of Concept**: Confirmed with a forge test (run, then deleted): `setParams(cap, 1 days, cooldown)`, open + close with a full-budget payout. `slash` at `closedAt + 1 day` reverts `NothingToSlash` (already fully vested, checked before the delay); `slash` at `closedAt + 2 days` (== SLASH_DELAY) also reverts `NothingToSlash`. No timestamp exists at which the term can be slashed.
**Recommendation**: Enforce `_streamLength > SLASH_DELAY` in both the constructor and `setParams` (e.g. `if (_streamLength <= SLASH_DELAY) revert StreamTooShort();`), or explicitly document that stream lengths at or below `SLASH_DELAY` opt the term out of slashability.

## [STAKE-3] Unbounded owner close timing lets the owner choose the mark window and defer stream start
**Severity**: Info
**Category**: evm-audit-defi-staking
**Location**: `closeTerm()` (ClawdIntern.sol:175-195)
**Description**: `closeTerm` requires `block.timestamp >= t.end` but has no deadline, and the stream starts at `closedAt`, not `t.end`. The owner can therefore (a) wait for a price dip so `markOut` — which the owner also supplies — floors the payout to zero, and (b) indefinitely defer the start of vesting for an already-earned payout, during which the intern's `lastTermEnd`/cooldown stamp is also pushed later (it is set to close time, not scheduled end). This is the vesting-escrow analog of the checklist's "early/delayed reward claims" [beirao LS-03]. It is already explicitly documented as part of the Phase 0 owner-trust surface (the `@dev` trust note on `closeTerm`), and it is self-limiting since no new term can open until close — hence Info, recorded so the trust assumption stays on the audit record.
**Proof of Concept**: Code path: open term, warp far past `t.end` without closing — `claimable` stays 0, `currentIntern` still reports the expired term, and a later `closeTerm(lowMark)` pays 0 while stamping `lastTermEnd = now`.
**Recommendation**: In the oracle phase, bind `markOut` to an observation window anchored at `t.end` (e.g. TWAP over `[end - w, end]`) and/or allow anyone to trigger close after `t.end + grace` so the stream clock and cooldown clock can't be deferred unilaterally.

## [STAKE-4] Cooldown is evaluated against the live global parameter, retroactively resizing an intern's lockout
**Severity**: Info
**Category**: evm-audit-defi-staking
**Location**: `openTerm()` (ClawdIntern.sol:133-134), `setParams()` (ClawdIntern.sol:250-256)
**Description**: Unlike `capBps` and `streamLen`, which are captured per-term at open, the cooldown check reads the *current* global `cooldown` against a `lastTermEnd` stamped in the past. Raising `cooldown` via `setParams` retroactively extends the lockout of every intern whose term already ended; lowering it (or zeroing it — the documented typo escape hatch) retroactively shortens all of them at once. This is consistent with "params for FUTURE terms only" (the cooldown gate is itself a future-open check) and everything here is owner-controlled, so there is no exploit — but it means the "captured at open so a param change never alters an open deal" guarantee does not extend to the cooldown half of the deal, which is worth having on the record.
**Proof of Concept**: `test_CancelPutsInternOnCooldown` in the existing suite already exercises the shortening direction (zero cooldown un-blocks a previously blocked intern immediately). The extending direction is symmetric: `setParams(cap, stream, 365 days)` after a close re-blocks an intern who was one second from eligibility.
**Recommendation**: None required for Phase 0; if cooldown is meant to be part of the per-term deal, stamp an absolute eligibility timestamp (`close time + cooldownAtClose`) at close/cancel instead of comparing against the live parameter.

## Coverage

Checklist areas mapped and checked (staking checklist → vesting-escrow analogs):

- **State machine (focus list)** — all combinations traced in code, several confirmed by scratch forge tests (4/4 passing, file deleted after):
  - *cancel-after-close*: impossible — `cancelTerm` reads `activeTermId`, which `closeTerm` atomically sets to `NONE`; a closed term can never be cancelled and a cancelled term (`closedAt == 0` forever) can never be closed, claimed, slashed, or reassigned. Terminal states are disjoint.
  - *double-close*: impossible (`NoActiveTerm` — `activeTermId == NONE`).
  - *slash-then-reassign* and *reassign-then-slash*: both allowed and both sound — confirmed slash at STREAM/4 then reassign then claim pays the heir exactly the vested remainder, contract drains to zero.
  - *claim-after-slash*: `_vested` returns the reduced `t.payout` for slashed terms; intern nets exactly vested-at-slash (also covered by existing tests).
  - *double-slash*: blocked by `TermAlreadySettled`.
  - *cooldown bypass via reassign*: **real — STAKE-1**.
- **Reward/payout calculation**: `gainBps` flooring documented in-contract; `payout <= budget` holds for all inputs (existing fuzz `testFuzz_PayoutMath`); `markIn == 0` and `capBps == 0` division guards present; `(markOut - markIn) * BPS` overflow requires markOut ~1e73 (owner-supplied, checked arithmetic, retryable) — no finding.
- **Vesting math**: `_vested` monotonic, capped at payout, no underflow (`closedAt <= now` whenever reachable); `claimed <= vested` invariant holds at slash time (claim sets `claimed = vested` at an earlier timestamp, vesting is non-decreasing until slash freezes it).
- **Balance accounting / overlapping streams**: invariant `balance >= Σ(payout_i - claimed_i) + activeBudget` verified under every path — openTerm pulls exactly `budget` (balance-delta check defeats fee-on-transfer), close keeps exactly `payout` and returns surplus, cancel returns exactly its own budget, slash returns exactly `payout - vested`, claim sends exactly `vested - claimed`. Confirmed by scratch test interleaving stream + half-claim + slash + a second locked-budget term + second close: exact conservation, zero dust, no shortfall (also covered by existing `test_CancelInterleavedBetweenStreamsConservesBalances`, `test_NewTermWhileOldStreamStillPaying`).
- **Params immutability per term**: `capBps`/`streamLen` captured at open and used exclusively from the Term struct in `closeTerm`/`_vested` — `setParams` mid-stream cannot alter an open term's deal (existing `test_ParamChangesDontTouchOpenTerm`). Cooldown is the exception — STAKE-4.
- **Reentrancy / CEI**: all token-moving functions `nonReentrant` with state written before transfer; `reassignIntern`/`setParams` make no external calls. No finding.
- **Stuck funds** [beirao LS-04]: denylisted intern → `reassignIntern` escape hatch exists; denylisted owner → close/cancel/slash stall until Ownable2Step transfer — already documented in the contract's trust-model header. `rescue` refuses CLAWD, `renounceOwnership` disabled. No new finding.
- **Not applicable**: all LSD-token items (stETH/rETH/cbETH/sfrxETH), validator/WithdrawCredentials items, inflation-attack/share-price items (no shares), `notifyRewardAmount` dilution (no reward-rate schedule), liquid-wrapper lock bypass (payout is address-bound, non-transferable; reassign is owner-only).
